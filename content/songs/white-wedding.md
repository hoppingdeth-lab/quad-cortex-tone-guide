veteran_notes: >-
  A 1985 track feature identifies a 1953 Gibson Les Paul, a 100-watt
  Marshall and a Marshall 4x12 for the recording. Stevens described
  the intro as a finger slide up the low E string over a sixteenth-note
  picking pattern; Keith Forsey then tracked the guitar twice. A later
  interview identifies the first-album guitar as a converted 1954
  Les Paul with PAF pickups, and the amp as a 1971 Marshall Super Lead
  100 with its matching cabinet.

  Brit Plexi 100 Bright is based on a Marshall Super Lead 100, so it is
  a close model-family choice. It does not reproduce a specific 1971
  amplifier or its recorded settings. The official Quad Cortex cabinet
  412 Brit 60B GB ’71 provides a period-appropriate Marshall 4x12
  approximation, but the original speakers are not identified.

  The contemporary track gear listing names guitar, amp and cabinet;
  it does not document an effect chain for this guitar part. This
  recipe therefore leaves the core tone dry. Doubler is an optional
  modern approximation of the documented double tracking, not an
  original effect or a parallel amp path. The serial order shown is
  the practical Quad Cortex build order.
