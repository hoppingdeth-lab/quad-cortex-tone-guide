sound: >-
  Aim for a bright, forward rock tone with enough gain to make full chords
  sound broad and energetic, while keeping individual notes distinct.
  The amp should provide most of the crunch. The optional Green 808 adds
  a little extra push for denser sections. Keep the sound lively and
  mid-present rather than extremely scooped or saturated.

beginner_route: >-
  Start with Brit 2203 and 412 Brit 60A G75 80s. Use the bridge pickup
  and set the amp for a firm crunch that still lets open chords ring
  clearly. Add Green 808 only if you want a little more sustain or push
  in the bigger sections. Use Simple Gate only if noise between parts
  is distracting.

veteran_notes: >-
  Guitar.com’s Riot!-era gear overview associates Farro with a
  Telecaster Deluxe, Marshall JCM2000 DSL and Mesa/Boogie Dual
  Rectifier, as well as a Boss DD-20 Giga Delay, DigiTech Whammy and
  volume pedal. These are useful era references, but they do not
  establish which guitar, amplifier, cabinet or effects were used on
  each track in the final studio recording.

  Neural DSP lists Brit 2203 as based on a Marshall JCM800. It is a
  supported stock Marshall-family choice, but its circuit is not the
  JCM2000 DSL. For a more Mesa-flavoured experiment, CA Tremo Modern
  is available and is based on a Mesa/Boogie Trem-O-Verb Red Modern;
  it is not a Dual Rectifier model. The selected Marshall cabinet is
  also a practical choice, not a confirmed original speaker cabinet.

  The Green 808 is included as an optional modern boost. It is not
  documented as a pedal Farro used on this song. The era gear lists
  mention delay and Whammy, but this recipe leaves them out of the
  core tone because they are not needed for the main rhythmic sound.

before_you_build: >-
  This is an original, ear-led Quad Cortex recipe inspired by the

  Start in standard E tuning and select the bridge pickup. If your
  chosen transcription or comparison with the record indicates another
  tuning, follow that source; tuning evidence for this track is not
  conclusive. Set the guitar volume fully open while dialling in the
  amp.

build_steps:
  - title: "Prepare the guitar"
    description: >-
      Begin in standard E tuning and use the bridge pickup. The
      track-specific tuning is not firmly established, so confirm
      against your transcription or the record before making a
      permanent setup change. Use clear, even strumming and keep
      chord changes controlled.

  - title: "Set the Brit 2203"
    description: >-
      Choose Brit 2203. As approximate starting points, try Gain
      around 5.5/10, Bass 4.5/10, Mid 6/10, Treble 6/10 and Presence
      5/10. Adjust the amp output or master to suit your monitoring
      level. Aim for a crunchy, responsive sound rather than maximum
      distortion.

  - title: "Add the optional Green 808"
    description: >-
      If the amp needs more push, place Green 808 before Brit 2203.
      Try Drive around 1.5/10, Tone near 5/10 and Level around 7/10
      as starting positions. This is a suggested boost setting, not
      a documented Farro setting. Bypass it if the sound gets
      compressed or too sharp.

  - title: "Choose the cabinet"
    description: >-
      Select 412 Brit 60A G75 80s. If the cabinet block offers
      microphone controls, begin with a dynamic microphone slightly
      off the speaker centre. Try a low cut around 80 Hz and a high
      cut around 8 kHz as initial targets. Adjust by ear; neither
      this cabinet nor these microphone choices are confirmed
      details of the original session.

  - title: "Set the optional gate"
    description: >-
      Enable Simple Gate only if the rig is noisy between phrases.
      Raise its threshold only far enough to clean up the gaps. Back
      it off if ringing chords or the ends of sustained notes are
      cut short.

scenes:
  - id: "A"
    name: "Lighter Rhythm"
    description: >-
      Brit 2203 and the cabinet active; Green 808 bypassed. Keep the
      amp at the lower end of the suggested gain range for lighter
      rhythmic sections. Use Simple Gate only if needed.

  - id: "B"
    name: "Full Rhythm"
    description: >-
      Keep the same amp and cabinet. Engage Green 808 with low Drive
      and higher Level for a firmer, more sustained sound in bigger
      sections. Adjust output to keep the scene’s volume consistent
      with Scene A.

troubleshooting:
  - problem: "Too harsh or fizzy?"
    solution: >-
      Lower Presence or Treble slightly, then try moving the
      cabinet microphone farther from the speaker centre. Avoid
      removing so much top end that the strumming loses its attack.

  - problem: "Too muddy?"
    solution: >-
      Reduce Bass on the amp or raise the cabinet low cut slightly.
      Check that the Green 808 is not adding excess low end or
      compression.

  - problem: "Not enough drive?"
    solution: >-
      Raise Brit 2203 Gain gradually. If you use Green 808, raise
      its Level before adding much Drive so the amp remains the
      main source of distortion.

  - problem: "Chords sound too compressed?"
    solution: >-
      Bypass Green 808 or reduce its Level. Lower the amp Gain a
      little and let the pick attack provide more of the sound.

  - problem: "Gate cuts off ringing chords?"
    solution: >-
      Lower the Simple Gate threshold or bypass it. The gate should
      clean up the gaps without shortening open chords.
